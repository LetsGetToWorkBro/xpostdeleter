Facebook

Here is the honest picture: Meta provides no API to delete posts from a personal timeline. PostCleaner automates what Meta actually allows, and gives you a proper guided path for the rest.

What is and isn't possible

No tool can honestly promise more than this without breaking Meta's terms.

Personal timeline posts cannot be deleted through any API. Meta removed publish_actions in Graph API v3.0 (2018) and never replaced it. The user_posts permission is read-only. Any tool claiming to bulk-delete your personal timeline automatically is either driving your logged-in browser session or calling private endpoints — both violate Facebook's Terms of Service and are a common cause of account locks. PostCleaner will not do that.
What PostCleaner does instead:
  • Fully automates deletion for Facebook Pages you administer — posts and your own comments, via the official Graph API.
  • Reads your “Download Your Information” export offline to show exactly how much is where, so you know which years to attack first.
  • Gives you a step-by-step Manage Activity walkthrough — Facebook's own bulk tool, 50 items at a time.

Delete a personal timeline, the supported way

Facebook's own bulk tool. Tedious, but it is the only method that will not put your account at risk.

Open Manage Activity

On desktop, go to your profile → ⋯ → Activity log → Manage activity → Your posts. On mobile it lives under Menu → Settings & privacy → Activity log.

Filter to one year at a time

Use the Date filter and start with your oldest year. Working year by year keeps the list short enough that the page stays responsive, and it is easy to track where you got to.

Deal with public posts first

Add the Audience → Public filter. Those are the posts anyone can find, so clearing them gives you most of the benefit for the least work.

Select and bin, 50 at a time

Facebook caps selection at 50 items per action. Tick the header checkbox to grab the visible batch, choose Bin (delete) or Archive (hide but keep), then repeat.

Empty the bin

Deleted items sit in the bin for 30 days before Facebook removes them for good. That is your undo window — and it means nothing is truly gone until you empty it or wait it out.

Comments and likes are separate

Switch the dropdown from Your posts to Comments, Likes and reactions, or Tags. Each has its own list and its own 50-at-a-time limit.

Browser extensions that click these buttons for you are automating your logged-in session, which Facebook's terms prohibit and its automated-behaviour detection looks for. If you use one, that is your call — but PostCleaner will not ship one.
